<StepCard steps={frontmatter.steps} anchor="demo">

Before each step the demo says what the command does and shows it in full. Press Enter to run it. Afterwards it sums up what the step wrote. The four steps find a <Term id="deviation">deviation</Term> between two releases, where every <Term id="value">value</Term> that differs counts, list every <Term id="revision">revision</Term> between them with its <Term id="class">class</Term>, and search for the revisions that cause the deviation.

<Callout tag="A term in plain words" title="Why exit status 1 is expected in step 2">

Every command reports with a number, its <Term id="exit-status">exit status</Term>, how it ended. `regression` reports 0 when the two results agree and 1 when they differ. Release N and N+1 differ on purpose, so 1 is the correct outcome here and not an error. The demo goes on.

</Callout>

<div slot="wide">
<Callout tag="A term in plain words" title="What “Not resolved” means" quote={notResolved('en')}>

Some changes are so small that each one alone moves the <span lang="de">Bruttobeitrag</span> by less than the tolerance of one cent per model point and month. Together they exceed it. DeltaRecon does not guess which of them is the cause: it names the group and leaves this part of the deviation unattributed.

</Callout>
